# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- You can pick a gnome up and carry it without killing it. Gnomes no longer take damage from bumps, drops or loot touching them (new setting: Sturdy gnomes). Weapons still hurt them.
- Loot is no longer damaged by bumping into a gnome.
- A carried valuable passes through other loot, doors and carts instead of knocking them about.
- Gnomes set things down gently on a free spot instead of on top of the pile, and never collide with anything a gnome has fetched.

## 2.0.0

- Gnomes fetch valuables and carry them to the closest player.
- New settings for fetch range, largest size, gnome speed and keep-away distance.

## 1.0.0

- Gnomes are passive.
